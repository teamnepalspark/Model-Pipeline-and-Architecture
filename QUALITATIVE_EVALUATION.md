# Qualitative Evaluation Protocol for Clinician Feedback

## Instructions for Clinicians

Please evaluate the distortion-corrected DWI images compared to the original distorted images.

### Evaluation Criteria:

1. **Distortion Correction Quality** (1-5 scale):
   - 1: No improvement, distortions still present
   - 2: Minimal improvement
   - 3: Moderate improvement
   - 4: Significant improvement
   - 5: Excellent correction, distortions eliminated

2. **Anatomical Alignment** (1-5 scale):
   - 1: Poor alignment with anatomical structures
   - 2: Slight misalignment
   - 3: Acceptable alignment
   - 4: Good alignment
   - 5: Perfect alignment

3. **Clinical Acceptability** (1-5 scale):
   - 1: Unacceptable for clinical use
   - 2: Poor quality
   - 3: Acceptable
   - 4: Good quality
   - 5: Excellent quality for clinical diagnosis

### Regions of Interest:
- Skull base
- Temporal lobes
- Other susceptible areas

### Comments:
Please provide any additional observations or concerns.

## Data Collection:
- Collect scores for each subject
- Compare baseline SOTA model vs. SwinUNETR model
- Analyze trends and statistical significance
